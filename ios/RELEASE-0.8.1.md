# Blasterball 0.8.1 (39)

BB2 Revolution, Remix and Holidays power-up corrections based on original code and artwork:

- Laser uses paired travelling bolts, the original quarter-second firing interval, shot speed and spacing, four-frame projectile animation, and red-paddle transformation. Hold the touch control or mouse button to fire; tapping/releasing requests a shot.
- Attach shows the original animated energy shield, keeps the ball at its contact position, and releases it on the launch gesture.
- Laser and Attach stay active until replaced or the life/stage ends, instead of expiring after 15 seconds.
- Catcher uses the original scrolling energy-field artwork and remains until it saves one ball.
- Original activation, deactivation, catch and laser recordings are connected. The original MPEG-in-WAV laser sample is decoded to PCM for Apple playback.
- Phantom behavior is preserved.

This remains a reconstruction. Adaptive geometry and touch controls are mobile adaptations, and full original-game parity is not claimed. The level editor and earlier campaign features remain included.

The public IPA is unsigned; sign it for your own device before installing.
