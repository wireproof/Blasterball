# Blasterball 0.8.0 (38) — prerelease

Adds an in-app Blasterball 2 level editor under the launcher Settings gear. Paint or erase bricks, assign drops, undo changes, save/open levels, import/export JSON, and play-test. Custom levels and scores are separate from the original campaigns. Imports receive fresh identities. This first editor does not author BB3 boss/polygon levels or import Windows binary world files.

Restores omitted BB2 power-ups including laser, blasterball, attach, catcher, flash, twin, phantom paddle, and eight-way. Phantom bricks disappear on hit and return; exploding bricks damage neighbors; plague bricks spawn additional bricks. BB3 Flash now clears enemies; Plague/Chain create bricks; Phantom responds to hits. Cherries produce fragments and pumpkins cause nearby damage, with original destruction artwork recovered from the game templates.

Revolution keeps its original artwork with cached 2x scaling and mild luminance sharpening for bricks, paddle, and backgrounds. Static-brick texture refreshes are eliminated; trails scale with multiball load; saves and HUD redraws are coalesced; BB3 overlay updates are reduced. These changes are not a physical-device performance guarantee.

The project remains a reconstruction. Original per-enemy AI, several BB3 abilities, exact effect timing/ranges, and the reported crescent-marked group show/hide mechanic are not fully verified. BB2's original Breaker/Exploder special effects are ball-hit behaviors; ordinary paddle contact retains its original burst.

Validation and delivery receipts are recorded separately. No physical-device launch is authorized for this campaign. Public IPA packages have development signing and embedded provisioning removed and require signing for the recipient's device.
