# Blasterball 0.8.2 (40)

Fixes the BB2 Twin (T) power-up's squashed, tiny paddles. Revolution, Remix and Holidays now use two normal-width paddle images with the original proportions and spacing. Twin resets inherited Mini/Grow sizing, stays active until replaced or the life/stage ends, and keeps both paddles inside the playfield. Ball, pickup and enemy contacts follow the visible paddles; the center gap remains open.

BB3 behavior is unchanged. This remains a reconstruction; the original Twin split/merge transition is not reproduced. The public IPA is unsigned and needs device signing.
